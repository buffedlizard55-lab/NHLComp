Methodology

This is a paper-trading research competition. No real money is wagered and this repository contains no order-placement code.

1. What the system does

It discovers, tests, forward-tests and paper-trades NHL betting strategies. Strategies are generated by the system itself from a search over point-in-time features, not supplied as a fixed list. Every wager is logged permanently with its price, its source and its verification status.

2. Point-in-time discipline

For a game at time T, features are built only from games that started strictly before T. This is enforced in features.FeatureBuilder._prior and re-checked by Backtester.assert_no_leakage, which flags any violation as a strategy_leakage irregularity. Elo ratings are seeded from the previous season's points percentage — never the current season's final standings.

3. Backtest versus forward test

The distinction is never blurred. A run is labelled BACKTEST only when the price it used was genuinely quoted in the past with a timestamp. Kalshi publishes hourly candlesticks for every contract (live tier for recent contracts, /historical tier for contracts settled before the cutoff); the closing price used here is the last hourly candle ending at or before the NHL scheduled start, and the open, T-24h, T-6h and T-1h candles give line movement. Such rows carry verification_status=kalshi_candle_close. A few early rows were priced from Kalshi's undocumented previous_yes_ask before the candle history was recovered; they keep the label single_source_timing_unverified permanently. Everything priced from a live quote is FORWARD TEST and settles later from the official NHL result. No sportsbook history exists in this system, so no backtest is ever claimed against sportsbook odds.

Two backtests are reported per strategy and never combined: an accuracy backtest on every decided game (hit rate, lift over base rate, log loss, Brier; data_sufficient=0, no P&L) and a priced backtest on the games whose Kalshi closing candle was recovered (flat 1-unit stakes at the offer, net of the exchange fee, on the whole window and on chronological train / validation / test splits). The accuracy backtest carries this caveat:

No verified historical betting-price feed is available, so this run measures predictive accuracy only. PnL, ROI and CLV are deliberately not computed -- doing so would require inventing historical odds. Profit is measured by the forward test instead.

3b. Markets traded

Moneyline (KXNHLGAME) is traded on both the backtest and the forward test. Totals (KXNHLTOTAL, "Over k.5 goals") is traded as follows, and the asymmetry is deliberate:

  • The line is read from the exchange's own floor_strike and strike_type fields — never parsed out of a title, because Kalshi words the same contract differently on the live tier ("Full Game: Over 8.5 goals scored") and the historical tier ("Carolina vs Vegas: Total Goals"). A contract with no readable line is skipped, not guessed.
  • An Over is the YES side, and the candlestick history publishes the YES offer, so an Over rule is backtested at real timestamped prices.
  • An Under is the NO side. The candlestick feed publishes only the YES bid/ask, so there is no historical NO offer to buy at, and no_ask = 1 − yes_bid is not assumed: on the quotes in this ledger that identity fails on 2 of 12 contracts (KXNHLGAME-26SEP20SJANA: yes_bid 0.19 against no_ask 0.99). An Under rule therefore carries a no_history_reason and is forward-test only, entered at the live no_ask_dollars Kalshi actually quotes. Its backtest row reports accuracy only, with data_sufficient=0 and no P&L.
  • Settlement uses the official final score. Kalshi counts regulation and overtime goals normally and counts a shootout as one goal for the winner, which is what the NHL's official score already contains — so the settled total is home + away with no adjustment. Every settled totals contract is also cross-checked against the exchange's own result, and a disagreement is recorded as a critical settlement_conflict rather than resolved.
  • Puck line (KXNHLSPREAD), first period (KXNHL1P), overtime (KXNHLOVERTIME) and player props are registered as series and researched, but nothing is bet on them: there is no period-level model and no verified prop feed. They stay in "waiting for other information" instead of being traded on an assumption.

3a. Data labels

  • SOURCE DATA — NHL schedule/results, stats REST per-game team and goalie lines, Kalshi quotes, settlements and candlesticks, the NHL partner-feed (DraftKings) odds, EDGE snapshots.
  • DERIVED — every feature (rest days, rolling PP%/PK%, shot share, PDO, starter save percentage, line movement, vig), every price point picked from a candle, every P&L figure.
  • MODEL OUTPUT — Poisson / Elo / logistic probabilities and any edge computed from them; also any third-party model number (e.g. xG) should one ever be ingested.
  • ASSUMPTION — recorded where used: the post-game goalie log identifies the starter for a past game (starters are announced pre-game, but this system did not observe the announcement); the fee model is Kalshi's published general schedule (M = 1 for KXNHLGAME); no slippage beyond the single quoted level.
  • UNVERIFIED — anything flagged in the Verification queue, and every strategy still carrying a blocked_reason.

4. Execution model

Buys happen at the offer, never the mid. Size is capped by the quoted yes_ask_size_fp; if the desired stake needs more contracts than are offered the order is partially filled and the remainder is recorded as unfilled. A missing or zero-size offer means no bet. Slippage is stored as 0 because only one book level is available — that is a limitation of the data, recorded rather than papered over. Fees: Kalshi's general taker fee, round up(0.07 × C × P × (1 − P)) per the fee schedule effective 2026-07-07 (KXNHLGAME is not on the non-standard list, so the multiplier is 1), is charged on every simulated fill and deducted from the settled P&L; the fee is stored on each bet row.

5. Staking and bankrolls

Model-based strategies use fractional Kelly, capped, with open exposure reserved from the bankroll so a strategy cannot over-commit. Market-structure rules (no model of their own) stake a flat fraction. Each strategy version has its own virtual bankroll; only FORWARD TEST results move it — BACKTEST P&L is reported separately and never funds a forward stake. Aggressive staking is permitted by design — the drawdown, volatility and losing-streak columns exist so the risk is visible, not so it is suppressed.

5a. Strategy versions and lifecycle

A strategy is identified by (strategy_id, version). Changing a rule creates a new version; the old one is set to retired with the reason, and its bets stay in the ledger. Lifecycle states: candidate → active → paused / retired / rejected, each transition logged in strategy_lifecycle with evidence. Blocked categories (no verified input yet) are registered with an explicit blocked_reason and stay in WAITING FOR OTHER INFORMATION rather than betting on an assumption.

5b. Sportsbook reference

The NHL partner feed publishes DraftKings moneylines for the current slate. They are stored as published (American odds), de-vigged proportionally, and attached to forward signals as a reference — Kalshi remains the execution venue. Because the feed has no history, the book-vs-exchange strategy is forward-test only and says so.

6. Statistics

Win rate is always shown with a 95% Wilson interval and a sample size. The discovery engine reports how many triggers it evaluated, so a survivor of a large search is not mistaken for a discovery. Models are compared against a home-ice constant baseline on a held-out window; a model that does not beat the baseline is not kept.

7. Auditability

Every fetched payload is stored with its URL, retrieval time and SHA-256 digest in raw_response. Every row carries a provenance label: SOURCE, DERIVED, MODEL, ASSUMPTION or UNVERIFIED. Bets are append-only; corrections go through amend_bet, which writes a before/after audit row.

8. Current state

78 strategies. FORWARD TEST: 81 simulated bets · 0 settled · 81 open · P&L 0.00 · ROI 0.0%. BACKTEST (reported separately, never merged): 3091 priced wagers · P&L 1,961.88 · ROI 1.9%.

9. Known limitations

  • No verified historical sportsbook odds. Closing-line value is measured against the Kalshi closing candle only; the DraftKings reference exists only from the day collection started.
  • The Kalshi closing candle is hourly: "close" means the last candle ending at or before the scheduled start, so it may include trades from the final minutes before puck drop. Kalshi's NHL books are thin outside the playoffs; the candle's yes_ask is a price, not a guarantee of size, and the fee model assumes the general schedule.
  • Starting goalies for upcoming games have no verified pre-game source, so goalie-gated strategies forward-test only when a starter becomes known; their backtests rely on the post-game log (an explicit ASSUMPTION).
  • Arena latitude/longitude is unavailable: the legacy NHL venue host could not be reached, so travel is derived from published venue UTC offsets rather than invented coordinates.
  • Line combinations, goalie announcements and player props have no verified feed. EDGE tracking is snapshotted forward only (season-to-date aggregates, no per-game history).
  • Preseason Kalshi markets are thin; contracts with zero size on both sides are recorded as insufficient_liquidity instead of being traded.
  • In-game candles (60/120 minutes after the start) are stored for research; the pipeline runs on a batch schedule and cannot execute during a game, so no in-game bet is simulated.
