Leaderboard

Two boards, never merged. Forward test is the competition: paper bets opened against a live Kalshi quote before puck drop, settled from the official result. Backtest is the same rules replayed against the recovered Kalshi closing candles (flat historical evidence, not live performance). Win rate is always paired with its 95% Wilson interval — a 3-bet sample proves nothing. Click any header to sort; click a username for its wagers. Filters combine with AND: strategy, market, team, player, goalie, season, month, metrics.
